# Cost-of-Living-in-Singapore
This is my first documented data analytics project. I aim to explore how prices for major categories have changed over time in Singapore.

## Core Project Question
How have prices for major categories changed over time in Singapore?

### Project Aims
1. Perform basic data cleaning using pandas and numpy.
2. Perform basic exploratory data analysis.
3. Analyse changes in major categories using weighted averages and suggest possible causes.
4. Provide visualisations to demonstrate the above findings.

### Project Description
The Consumer Price Index (CPI) is designed to measure the average price changes of a fixed basket of consumption goods and services commonly purchased by resident households over time and is widely used as a measure of consumer price inflation. It is measured by taking a weighted average of the percentage price changes of each item in this basket of goods and services.

In the CPI, the goods and services are broadly classified into ten main divisions, namely:
1. Food
2. Clothing & Footwear
3. Housing & Utilities
4. Household Durables & Services
5. Health
6. Transport
7. Information & Communication
8. Recreation, Sport & Culture
9. Education
10. Miscellaneous Goods & Services

Two types of data are required for the compilation of the CPI, i.e. weights and prices of the selected goods and services. The CPI reflects the aggregated effect of changes in the prices and the associated weights for the goods and services monitored.

In the compilation of the CPI, weights reflect the relative importance of each good or service in the basket, that is, their share as a proportion of total household expenditure. Households tend to spend more on some goods and services, and less on others. As a result, various goods and services with the same price movements will have different impacts on the overall CPI due to their differing weights. The weights used in the CPI are derived from the expenditure values collected in the Household Expenditure Survey (HES) conducted by DOS.
